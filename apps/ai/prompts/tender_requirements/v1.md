- summary: two sentences: what the buyer wants done or supplied, and by whom.

- sections: the parts of the technical proposal the bidder writes (approach and
  methodology, work plan, team, experience…), with the tender's form names. Not the
  financial proposal, and not forms that are only filled in and signed.
- evaluation_criteria: how the technical proposal is scored, with points or weights.
- documents_required: attachments asked for that are not already a mandatory
  requirement.
- key_dates: clarification deadline, pre-bid meeting, site visit, closing, opening,
  each as YYYY-MM-DD with the time when given.
- submission: where and how bids go (portal or address), copies, envelopes, binding,
