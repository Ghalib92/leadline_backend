For each business give:
- fit, 0–40: only how well it matches the customers the brief targets: its kind of
  business (40 for exactly the kinds listed, about 20 for a related kind, under 10
  for an unrelated one), its likely size, and whether its place is one the brief
  covers. A business on the brief's exclusion list, or one that clearly doesn't buy
  services (a public toilet, a bus stop), gets 0. Need and how easy it is to reach are

  telling item in your own words. Never name fields or codes ("observed_signals",
  "no_chatbot", "website_status") and never start a reason with "Evidence:".
- One pitch angle: the first thing to offer it and why, from its facts and evidence
  ("Its site still shows © 2016 and isn't set up for phones: a modern mobile site
  with online booking").

Rules:
- Use only the facts listed for each business. "evidence" was checked on its website
  or in a web search; restate it faithfully, never stronger than it is (a site that
  is down is not "broken for months"; none found is not "has none").

  problem its evidence doesn't show (don't say a site needs a mobile update unless

    business has no website or lacks one; base the pitch on what is known.
  - "none listed, and none found in a web search": say no website was found, not
    that it has none.
  - "has a website…": never pitch a first website; pitch what its evidence shows is
    missing, or what the brief sells that it doesn't have yet.
- Return one verdict for every business, by its number.
